DUSTFLOW - The Beginning Of A Conklin Factory Training Platform

Before you say anything, I do sleep. You know who doesn't sleep? My Claude agents!

I developed DUSTFLOW for the laminate shop first, since I designed these systems and know them inside and out. It's interactive 3D training: each person works through the real procedure, step by step, on a full model of the shop, the dust collector and its controls. It runs in English, Español (Spanish), Français (French) and Kiswahili (Swahili), with up to three shown at once. From here the training only grows: I can now build a 3D model of a shop from phone pictures. If you're interested and like the idea, I'd like to go forward on completing the training software.

LAUNCH — wesprojects.github.io/DUSTFLOW

WHAT IT DOES
  • A true-to-scale 3D model of the shop, the collector and every control.
  • One action per step. The exact button, key, lever or hatch glows, and only that control responds.
  • Each step says who does it and what they should see, so every person knows their part.
  • Checks at the end must be 100% right, with retries.
  • Runs in a browser on any shop PC, no install, and updates itself.
The training library: pick languages, then a training.
EXAMPLE 1: DUST COLLECTOR START-UP

Starters 1, 2, 3, checked at the airlock box and by listening. The camera stays on the starter wall; the blower and fan run in a corner window.

1. Press the green button on starter 1. This starts the rotary airlock.
2. Check the airlock box.
3. Press the green button on starter 2. This starts the transfer blower.
4. Listen for the transfer blower.
5. Press the green button on starter 3. This starts the dust collector.
6. Listen for the dust collector.
EXAMPLE 2: WEEKLY FLAMEX TEST

Conklin's weekly testing process, with three people. The interior and exterior systems are tested one at a time.

Interior system: prep
1. Turn the key on the Interior Flamex Panel to bypass (1).
Employee 1
2. Open the hatch over the CNC machine and clean out the debris. Click it twice: open, then clean and close.
Employee 3
3. Open the hatch near the window and clean out the debris. Click it twice: open, then clean and close.
Employee 2
4. Turn the key back to active (0).
Employee 1
Interior system: test
5. Make sure the key on the Interior Flamex Panel is at active (0).
Employee 1
6. Open the hatch near the window.
Employee 2
7. Place the bucket under the open hatch.
Employee 2
8. Open the hatch over the CNC and shine the flashlight in to trip the system.
Employee 3
9. Check: Fire LED on, buzzer, horns and strobes on, water sprays into the bucket.
Employee 1 and 2
10. Once it has tripped, shut the CNC hatch.
Employee 3
11. Turn the key to bypass (1).
Employee 1
12. Press ext.
Employee 1
13. Press reset.
Employee 1
14. Turn the key back to active (0).
Employee 1
15. Once it has drained and been wiped with a cloth, shut the hatch.
Employee 2
16. Take the bucket away.
Employee 2
Exterior system: test
17. Use the ladder to unhook the hose from the dumpster.
Employee 1
18. Go to the side of the shed with the hatch and tell Employee 2: "ready check".
Employee 1
19. Open the riser hatch and shine the light in to trip the system. Close the hatch once it trips.
Employee 1
20. At the Exterior Flamex Panel: turn the key to bypass (1).
Employee 2
21. Press ext.
Employee 2
22. Press reset.
Employee 2
23. Turn the key back to active (0).
Employee 2
24. Press the grey int button.
Employee 2
25. Use the ladder to reconnect the hose to the dumpster.
Employee 1
Done
