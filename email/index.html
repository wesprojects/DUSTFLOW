CONKLIN OFFICE FURNITURE - RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

Interactive 3D training for the laminate shop dust collection at 75 Appleton. Trainees work in a full 3D model of the shop, the collector shed and its controls, and do each procedure step by step on the real equipment: starters, Flamex panels, hatches, blast gates and lockout points. Every training runs in English, Español (Spanish), Français (French) and Kiswahili (Swahili), with up to three shown at once. These first trainings cover the dust collection system; the same format will carry the rest of the factory's procedures.

START
  1. Open the launch link in Chrome or Edge on any office PC. No install or account.
  2. The training library opens first. Every training shows as a card.
  3. Pick 1 to 3 languages. The first is shown large, the others under it.
  4. Optional: enter a training pin to show only the trainings assigned to that team.
  5. Click a card to start.
The training library: language buttons, training pin, one card per training.
HOW A TRAINING WORKS

Each step shows one card on the right:

  • The instruction — one action, in plain words.
  • Who does it — Employee 1, 2 or 3.
  • You should see — what the equipment does when the step is done right.
  • Back and Repeat.

The control for the step glows in the 3D model, and only that control responds. Anything else gives a red X: “Not this one.” The camera moves to each control on its own. A correct action shows a green check and the training moves on. Check questions at the end must all be right, with retries. The last screen reads TRAINING COMPLETE!

EXAMPLE 1: WEEKLY FLAMEX TEST

Built from Conklin's Flamex Weekly Testing Process. The interior and exterior systems are tested one at a time. Every button is pressed on the 3D panel itself, laid out like the real one on the wall.

Interior system: prep
  1. Click the glowing Interior Flamex Panel. Its clear cover swings open.
  2. Turn the key to bypass. You should see: Supervisory light on.
Step 1: the panel glows; the card says what to do and what you should see.
The cover is open; the key glows on the panel face.
  1. Open the hatch over the CNC machine, clean out the debris, close it.
  2. Open the hatch near the window, clean out the debris, close it.
  3. Turn the key back to active.
The camera goes to each hatch; the hatch to click glows on the duct.
Interior system: test
  1. Open the hatch near the window and place the bucket under it.
  2. Shine the flashlight into the CNC hatch. The system trips: Fire light, buzzer, horns, strobes, water.
  3. Shut the CNC hatch. The water keeps spraying.
  4. At the panel: key to bypass, press ext (horns stop), press reset (water stops), key back to active.
  5. Once drained and wiped, shut the window hatch and take the bucket away.
The trip: water sprays across the duct at the window nozzle.
Exterior system: test
  1. Use the ladder to unhook the hose from the dumpster.
  2. At the riser hatch by the shed: call “ready check”, open the hatch and shine the light. The system trips.
  3. At the Exterior Flamex Panel: key to bypass, ext, reset, key back to active, then the grey int button.
  4. Reconnect the hose to the dumpster.
The Exterior Flamex Panel: the button to press (ext) glows.
The end screen: Restart or Exit back to the library.
EXAMPLE 2: DUST COLLECTOR START-UP AND SHUT-DOWN

Starters 1, 2, 3 to start, 3, 2, 1 to stop. The camera stays on the starter wall the whole time. The machines are shown in a small window in the corner.

Start-up
  1. Press the green button on starter 1. This starts the rotary airlock.
  2. Check the airlock box: green light on, the screen reads SYSTEM ON.
  3. Press the green button on starter 2. This starts the transfer blower.
  4. Listen for the transfer blower. The corner window shows the blower wheel turning.
  5. Press the green button on starter 3. This starts the dust collector.
  6. Listen for the dust collector. The corner window shows the main fan turning.
The starter wall: starter 1 glows.
Listening step: the transfer blower runs in the corner window; the camera stays on the starters.
Shut-down
  1. Press the red button on starter 3. The dust collector slows and stops.
  2. Press the red button on starter 2. The transfer blower stops.
  3. Press the red button on starter 1.
  4. Check the airlock box: red light on, the screen reads SYSTEM OFF.

Three check questions follow: the start order, the shut-down order, and where the rotary airlock is started.
